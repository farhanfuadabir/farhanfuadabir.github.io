Research

Multimodal Ultrasound Intelligence

Current · PhD

A pipeline that pairs B-mode ultrasound with a small set of clinical features to help radiologists work through Phyllodes tumors and pleural effusions. Two things make it hard: the tumors are rare, so labels are thin, and a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.

  • Feature-level fusion. Dual-branch encoders learn from ultrasound sweeps and structured vitals separately, then cross-attention finds where the two agree.
  • A trust layer. CAM and Grad-CAM++ saliency overlays tuned for sonographers, so a clinician can see which region drove the call.
  • Realistic data. A diffusion module synthesizes B-mode variants for the minority classes, which eases the imbalance without teaching the model to memorize.

The whole thing runs in a Gradio review studio where physicians can scrub frames, toggle saliency, and audit the clinical context behind each prediction.

Ultrasound Landmark Detection

Current · PhD

YOLOv11-based neck landmark localization with class-aware sampling, meant to guide ultrasound-driven procedures. The sampling matters more than the architecture here, since the interesting landmarks are the ones that show up least often.

The same thread led to real-time median nerve and vessel tracking that runs on resource-constrained devices, led by Mahad Ali and presented at EMBC 2026.

Neuro-Symbolic RL Distillation

M.S. thesis

My master's work on pulling editable symbolic policies out of black-box reinforcement learning agents, advised by Dr. Rickard Ewetz.

  • LLM-assisted feature carving. Prompting language models with environment specs and replay buffers to derive compact feature dictionaries for visual and continuous observation spaces.
  • Neural-guided extraction. A teacher policy supervises a differentiable decision list constrained by temporal logic templates. Gradients and SAT-based pruning together keep the rules short enough for a human to read.
  • Editable safety envelopes. Because the output is made of understandable predicates, a domain expert can patch a behavior without retraining anything.

It tracks high-performing agents on MuJoCo and gridworld tasks while showing what the policy actually attends to, which is the part I care about for safety-critical autonomy.

PCovNet and PCovNet+

Qatar University

A CNN-VAE framework with LSTM embeddings that detects the earliest physiological signs of COVID-19 from commodity smartwatches.

  • Learns a per-user baseline from raw PPG, accelerometer, and heart-rate traces, so it does not need a population average to compare against.
  • Hybrid reconstruction and prediction losses catch both slow drift and sudden spikes.
  • LSTM embeddings hold onto circadian rhythm, which cuts false alarms when someone changes their schedule or starts working out.
  • Light enough to run on a mobile gateway with federated fine-tuning.

In longitudinal trials it flagged presymptomatic cases up to 36 hours before PCR confirmation.

Hardware and open source

ASL-DataGlove

Embedded ML · Wearables

A low-cost glove that reads American Sign Language gestures in real time. It began as my undergrad capstone and now does duty at outreach demos.

  • Custom hardware. A light, fabric-friendly glove with a flex sensor per finger, a 9-DOF IMU, and haptics for two-way cues. The board can be fabricated locally for under $35.
  • Datasets. Two datasets covering 14 static and 3 dynamic gestures from 35 participants, capturing the between-person variation that most public sets flatten out.
  • Modeling. A time-frequency embedding plus CNN pipeline, and a CNN-LSTM hybrid that runs on-device through TensorFlow Lite.

It recognizes gestures in under 40 ms at better than 92% accuracy, and it is open source for community labs. The work became our Scientific Reports paper and won Best Paper at ICIEV 2021.
