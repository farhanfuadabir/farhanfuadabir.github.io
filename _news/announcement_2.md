I officially started the Computer Engineering PhD at UCF this fall under the mentorship of Dr. Laura Brattain. I am grateful to receive the ORCGS Doctoral Fellowship, which gives me the freedom to focus on building trustworthy multimodal AI for ultrasound diagnostics.

My current projects include:

- Feature-level fusion models that combine B-mode ultrasound imagery with a minimal set of clinical biomarkers.
- CAM-based explanation tooling tailored for breast and pleural effusion workflows.
- Diffusion-driven augmentation pipelines that rebalance highly skewed ultrasound datasets.

If you are interested in collaborating on explainable imaging AI or ultrasound acquisition systems, please reach out!
